## Observed failures before edits

1. **High — paid order did not automatically enter the pickup Driver queue.**
   - Reproduce: create and pay for a local mock Customer order, then open the Driver pickup queue.
   - Observed: no `driver_assignments` row existed and the Driver queue was empty.
   - Likely root cause: payment capture completed the order but never called the active logistics assignment path.

2. **High — new Customer orders could have no assigned Facility.**
   - Reproduce: create an order, pick it up, and open the Driver handoff screen.
   - Observed: `Assigned facility unavailable`, and no facility destination could be opened.
   - Likely root cause: order creation did not select an active facility with valid coordinates.

3. **High — Start Facility Transit did not open directions.**
   - Reproduce: complete pickup and tap Start Facility Transit.
   - Observed: the assignment state changed but Maps directions did not open.
   - Likely root cause: the shared facility-handoff helper started transit without calling the facility directions helper.

4. **Medium — Driver handoff showed only a QR code.**
   - Reproduce: open the facility handoff section.
   - Observed: the visual QR had no visible order number fallback.
   - Likely root cause: the driver detail mapping omitted `orderNumber`.

5. **High — Facility intake supported QR/code entry but not Order ID search.**
   - Reproduce: open Scan Incoming Order on a device where the camera is unavailable.
   - Observed: staff could not accept the order using the visible `BW-\" order number.
   - Likely root cause: the intake API exposed only handoff-code resolution.

6. **Medium — Facility dashboard and verification content overflowed on Android.**
   - Reproduce: open the Facility work queue and garment verification on the shown emulator width.
   - Observed: the Incoming badge and order status rank outside their cards.
   - Likely root cause: rows used non-wrapping content without flex shrink constraints.

7. **High — Parcel ID was customer-visible and required for final packing.**
   - Reproduce: complete Wash, Dry, Iron, Fold, and Pack, then confirm packing.
   - Observed: staff had to enter a parcel value and internal operator/parcel details were displayed.
   - Likely root cause: the mobile contract and DTO made `parcelId` required even though it is only an internal audit value.

8. **Blocker — Facility Staff could not finish QC or move an order to Ready.**
   - Reproduce: as an active Facility Staff member, complete all processing and packing, then try to pass QC.
   - Observed: the app said a Facility Manager must perform QC and the role was rejected at UI, API, and database function layers.
   - Likely root cause: manager-only guards existed in three layers.

9. **High — delivery auto-assignment did not follow Ready/QC.**
   - Reproduce: complete final packing and QC.
   - Observed: no delivery assignment was created.
   - Likely root cause: the QC success path did not call the logistics delivery assignment path.

## Pre-fix conclusion

The Customer UI and catalogue were working at the audited baseline. The failures were in the server-authoritative cross-role handoff chain and in Facility/Driver presentation. The audit was completed and these failures were documented before any fix was made.

## Additional delivery proof finding

10. **High — iOS delivery proof exposed only camera capture.**
    - Reproduce: on an iOS simulator, or a phone whose camera is unavailable/denied, open an arrived delivery and tap `Take delivery photo`.
    - Observed: the flow stopped with `Unable to take delivery photograph` and offered no photo-library fallback.
    - Root cause: the Driver delivery service imported only `launchCamera`, while the established claims flow already supported `launchImageLibrary`. The delivery screen presented only the camera action.
